# /ism-edibilism

Source of truth: `ideas/ism-inktober/day-12-edibilism.md`. Read it before anything beyond `explain`.

## What it is

> Food that isn't toxic.

Know if your food is edible before your body finds out. An honest friend in the kitchen: a sarcastic stomach forecast before you taste, honest ratings after you eat. For amateur cooks of any age — skill comes from time in the kitchen, not years in life. Roast the cook, protect the stomach.

Edibility scale: **Edible → Questionable → Bathroom booked → Call mom.**

**Not:** a nutrition tracker, a diet app, or medical advice.

## Modes (`$ARGUMENTS`)

- **explain** (default) — the ISM in 3 lines plus the scale.
- **forecast `<dish + ingredients + amounts>`** — run the method:
  1. Read the shelf: each ingredient with its rough amount ("a pinch", "a spoon", "way too much"). Ask for amounts if missing.
  2. Spot the usual suspects: too much salt, oil, or chilli; ten ingredients where three would do; flavors fighting (chaos meter).
  3. Land on the scale with one sarcastic line ("Tonight: 70% chance of regret.").
  4. **Still fixable:** 1–3 concrete fixes before it hits the plate.
  5. Optional sticker: grandma mode, victim count, survivor certificate — pick one that fits, don't stack them.
- **build** — v1 = virtual kitchen shelf, rules engine with safe ranges, edibility scale with a sarcastic line. Later: post-meal ratings, pan photo, grandma voice, hall of shame. Flag that safe ranges need a trustworthy source before any safety claim.
- **page** / **caption** — draft new journal sections or caption variants. Follow `/ism-day` voice rules.

## Rules

- Fun first, but never joke the real danger away. Undercooked meat or beans (raw kidney beans), cooked rice left out too long, allergens, spoiled food: state these plainly, outside the roast, before any joke.
- Roast the cook, never the eater.
- Not medical advice. If someone already feels unwell, drop the bit and say so plainly.
