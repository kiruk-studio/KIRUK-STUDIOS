# Sketch layer

## What the founder said

- Making everything sketch-based is very difficult, and people might not like it or understand it clearly.
- So the **base** is a normal font and a normal, generated or illustrated visualization: the wireframe, the idea of what the design is trying to show, the prototype.
- Then a **hidden layer or top layer**: our own sketch on top, drawn by hand on the graphic tablet — pencil stroke, or a stroke of a different type.
- When it scales up or down it must still look right at every size.
- "We are portraying our idea on top of our imagination."
- Documents will be uploaded too.

## Settled (plan §1)

- Base layer stands alone; the sketch layer only adds.
- Sketch layer is vector, pinned to base elements, visible by default.

## Open

- Reveal: always on, or can the visitor hide/show it?
- Pinning: how a stroke knows which base element it belongs to, so it moves and scales with layout.
- Stroke types: which ones (pencil, pen, marker, ink?) and when each is used.
- Authoring flow: tablet → which app → SVG export → where it lands in the repo.
