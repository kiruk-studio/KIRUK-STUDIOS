# dawn-hero — notes

**Tried:** one composed landing frame. The founder's logogram rises on the horizon at dawn: above the line it is a Da Vinci notebook study (sepia ink, left-hand hatching, construction circles, a 24-hour dial, typewritten notes); below the line the same mark continues as a wireframe (nodes, dimensions, glow) on a perspective ground converging at the sun. Sky and earth colours sampled from the references. Reading B (toggle) puts the whole drawn mark in the sky and its built reflection in the earth, labels mirrored like Leonardo's script.

**Worked:** the logogram already has two halves, a centre line and needles 15° apart, so "sketch above, build below" falls out of the mark itself. Courier Prime with the static bleed filter carries the typewriter-ink feel at title size. Contrast passes everywhere (lowest: notes on the rose band, 5.1:1).

**Didn't:** static ink in the sky. Three tries read as a grey smudge, a cutout cloud, then smoke. Layered translucent washes are the best still version; real ink needs motion — TSL workstream. Phones hide it.

**Open:** founder's read at a glance; reading A or B; a reference image for "ink splashed in the sky".

**Caveat:** Google Fonts (unpinnable) for the proto only.
