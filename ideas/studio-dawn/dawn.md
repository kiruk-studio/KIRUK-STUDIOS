# Dawn

_Arun means dawn in Tamil._

## What the founder said

- The calm dawn before sunrise, with a thin layer of rays shown before the sun comes. That dawn is the palette.
- Dawn is the design part — where new things are brought to life, the start of something of our own.
- Look at a dawn landscape: the **bottom is darker** (mountain, landscape, building — any part of earth), the **top, the sky, is lighter**. A ray of light comes, also lighter. Light collides with the dark, and then there is the shade. Overall it mingles, the way dawn comes before sunrise.
- The dawn is not there all the time. It comes eventually, with the ray. "When something is going to happen, the dawn will come." That is where the work is shown.
- It is a loop: the sun comes up and goes down. At night we show the "nightminds" — something shown when everything is dark. As it rises we show the idea, and then the clear idea of how it will look. Dawn is the main moment.
- "Even I'm not clear about the idea, so let's break it down."
