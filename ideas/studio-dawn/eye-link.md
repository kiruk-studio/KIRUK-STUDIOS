# Eye link

## What the founder said

- The design is all about the eye, at first.
- The logo: each needle shows a time angle — timeless design. It points in 8 directions — we give the right direction. The shape looks like an eye — the visionary idea behind everything. The line shows our support.
- The logo is not final. Use the current one for now; a better idea and a better logo may come later.
- The full-eye design (a giant eye over everything) breaks everything and is not clear. Use the eye, but carefully.
- "The ink bleed and the stroke of a scribble hold the eye, to show the overflowing creativity."
