# Sheet I — the studio home, measured

Claim: The page is a mark, a word, and a sentence, held on a baseline, and the red line says which distances sit on the module.

Instrument: pen. The logogram paths are copied from the favicon and drawn unfiltered; the word and the claim are set in Fell.

The drawing is NTS. The red numbers are the live CSS at a 16px root. `Z1`, `S1`, `T1`, and `L1` are off the golden-ratio module. The four ticks marked `lg` are 16px at true size.

Source: `apps/kiruk-studio/src/app/globals.css`, `(site)/page.tsx`. The CSS was not changed.

Skills: `kiruk-sheet`, `kiruk-rule`, `kiruk-stroke`, checked with `kiruk-codex`.
