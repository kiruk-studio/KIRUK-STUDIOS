## Concept

When you showcase a product, a brand, or yourself, consistency alone doesn't create connection. Inviteism is a way to invite someone with a customized message, illustration, or page made only for them, blending your brand with their character, so they feel cared for. Template messages feel AI-generated and sloppy; this brings trust back.

## The method (as used by `/ism-inviteism`)

1. **Their half** — what they care about, how they talk, what they're chasing. Only from what you know or what they've made public on purpose.
2. **My half** — my brand, my story, what I offer.
3. **The meeting point** — the one place both halves overlap. If there isn't one, don't send.
4. **The invite** — a message, illustration, or one-person page built around that meeting point.
5. **The rule** — AI can help make it; it can't be the reason it's made.
