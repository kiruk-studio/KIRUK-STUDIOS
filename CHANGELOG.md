- **The eye, lit, constructed, measured and annotated (2026-11-04).** The engraved eye on `apps/kiruk-studio` `/` now has five new parts:
  - **Light.** One light, moved by the pointer, lights both the paper and the eye. Each region has a form (the globe, the lid rolling over it, the hollow under the brow, the brow ridge, the lid's pillow), and its lines widen in shade and thin in light. The lid casts onto the white, the lashes cast a broken band, and the brow shades the hollow. The corneal window follows the light.
  - **Construction.** The whole globe is drawn behind the lids, with its meridians turning with the gaze, the optic axis, the orbit and the centre lines. It is red chalk on the old paper and blueprint blue on the new, with hidden lines dashed. It can be faint, full or hidden (W).
  - **Measures.** A live layer holds W (the opening, with its 3.1° tilt), A (the aperture, through a blink), p (the pupil, through the light reflex), and a 10 mm bar that gives the figure's drawn scale. That scale changes with the layout. Four units cycle with U (d, mm, px, Paris lines), and calipers (M) snap to the anatomy.
  - **Notes.** Seven notes run in a chain, i to vi: scribble, measure, light, paper, water, make. [Pretext](https://github.com/chenglou/pretext) lays them out into whatever room the layout leaves: the left column, into the curve under the eye, sideways up the margin, along the foot, and in mirror script over the brow. Rubric leaders tie each one to its part.
  - **Instrument.** A new _vi. Measure_ section holds the unit, the scale, the axis angles, the light, the calipers and the construction.

- **The dawn: two papers, and an ink bleed (2026-11-04).** The engraved eye on `apps/kiruk-studio` `/` is now printed on one of two papers.
  - **Dawn Light** `#fadfa9`, the old paper. It is where the hand drew: it takes iron-gall ink and carries its years.
  - **Dawn Departs** `#ccffff`, the new paper. It is where the code draws: it takes dawn navy and has no age.

  The founder chose both from names.color.pizza. Arun means dawn, and the plate now shows the hour between the hand and the code. Switching papers (title block, the instrument's _iv. Dawn_, or D) brings the new sheet across as a front that wicks along the fibres, not a fade. One spring value, `--dawn`, drives the GPU paper, the inks, the wash and the CSS type together, so paper and ink always change together.

  Dragging across the plate wets it. Water diffuses through the paper's own absorbency, lifts ink off the lines and the type, and dries darkest at its rim. The stain fades with a half-life of about 11 s. The instrument plots the wetness and its rate (_v. Ink_).

  Type moved to the Fell family for the plate (IM Fell English, plus IM Fell English SC for the plate number) and Xanh Mono for measures. Printed type now separates ink, which multiplies into the sheet, from body colour, which is laid on top, so paper samples keep their true colour on either sheet. The no-GPU poster is re-baked as ink on white so it multiplies onto either paper.

  Tokens are proposed in [`openspec/changes/dawn-paper-tokens`](openspec/changes/dawn-paper-tokens/proposal.md). That adds `color.dawn.*` (papers, inks, rubric) and a scoped `color.sheet.*` semantic group, with chroma, contrast and pairing limits. The change validates `--strict`, and `tokens:build` is clean. Concept: [`ideas/studio-dawn/eye.md`](ideas/studio-dawn/eye.md); model: skill `kiruk-burin`, `references/bleed.md`.

## How to write an entry

1. New change? Add under `## [Unreleased]` → one of `Added` / `Changed` / `Removed` / `Fixed` / `Decisions`.
2. Each bullet: what changed + **Why**: line explaining intent.
3. On release (brand v1, phase completion, etc.): rename `Unreleased` → `[vX.Y] — YYYY-MM-DD`, start a fresh `[Unreleased]` on top.
4. Plan shifts (roadmap edits, scope drops) MUST land here, not in the roadmap diff alone.
